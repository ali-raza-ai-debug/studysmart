Which subjects do you cover?

All subjects, including Business Management, Computer Science, Law, Accounting & Finance, Economics and Statistics, plus thesis and dissertation support, Excel and SPSS. Message us with your subject and we'll tell you how we can help.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the subject, the type of help and your deadline. Tell us what you need on WhatsApp and you'll get a clear price before anything starts.

Mostly online, so we can help students anywhere in Pakistan.

Can you build a chatbot for my business?

Yes. We build AI chatbots that answer from your own documents, FAQs and prices, for your website or WhatsApp, and connect them to your sheets, email or CRM.
